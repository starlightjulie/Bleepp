# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### New
- **Calls ring like calls.** When an app like Google Voice is ringing for a call and your phone is silent, Bleepp keeps ringing until you answer, decline or miss it, for up to a minute. Each app has its own call sound, and it starts on your phone's ringtone.
- **Filters tab.** Everything about what the log keeps now lives in one place, as a fourth tab.
- **Block a feature.** Stop logging one kind of notification from an app, like Charging, without blocking the whole app. Do it from the Filters tab or straight from a notification's details.
- **Silent updates collapse.** Charging, downloads and timers that update the same notification over and over now show as one entry with the newest text.
- **Hidden words.** Notifications with any of the words you choose aren't logged.
- **Bookmarks.** Star apps so they come first in the log's filters, and bookmark single notifications so they're never cleaned up. A Saved filter shows them all.
- **Swipe and select.** Swipe an entry left to delete it (with Undo) or right to bookmark it. Long-press to select several at once.
- **Details sheet.** See a notification's full text, which feature it came from, copy it, or stop logging that kind.
- **Pause alerts.** For an hour, until the morning, or until you turn them back on. There's a Quick Settings tile for it, and a Pause button on every alert.
- **Backup and export.** Back up your alerts, filters, settings and look to a file and restore them on another phone. Export the whole log as a spreadsheet file.
- **Lock the log.** Optionally ask for your fingerprint or screen lock before the log shows, and hide it from the recent apps screen.
- **Ring sound.** A new built-in classic double ring.

### Improved
- **License notice.** About now shows the copyright and no-warranty line, and tapping it opens the license terms.
- **Undo stays longer.** Messages with an Undo button stay for 10 seconds, and messages sent while a file picker is closing wait until you're back.

## 1.0.0

### New
- **Silent alerts.** Give any app its own sound that plays when your phone is on silent or vibrate. It stays out of the way when your phone can ring.
- **Break through Total Silence.** Let the apps that really matter sound even in Total Silence Do Not Disturb. Off by default, and set per app.
- **Your own sounds.** Pick from 8 built-in sounds or use an audio file, with a volume for each app.
- **Keyword rules.** Only sound when a notification mentions the words you choose.
- **Repeat until seen.** Keeps sounding until you open or clear the notification, or tap Stop.
- **Quiet hours.** Keep alerts quiet on a schedule, with a minimum gap between alerts from one app.
- **Notification log.** Every notification with its app, time and message. Search it, filter by app, and tap an entry to jump to where it pointed.
- **Lighthouse look.** 16 colourways, your own colours and saved looks, 6 animated backgrounds, and 6 app icons.
- **23 languages.** Including right-to-left layouts.
- **Update pop-up.** Tells you when a new official version is out, with what's new. Settings also shows whether your copy is an official build.
