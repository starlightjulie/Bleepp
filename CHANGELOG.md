# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### New
- **Silent alerts.** Give any app its own sound that plays when your phone is on silent or vibrate. It stays out of the way when your phone can ring.
- **Break through Total Silence.** Let the apps that really matter sound even in Total Silence Do Not Disturb. Off by default, and set per app.
- **Your own sounds.** Pick from 8 built-in sounds or use an audio file, with a volume for each app.
- **Keyword rules.** Only sound when a notification mentions the words you choose.
- **Repeat until seen.** Keeps sounding until you open or clear the notification, or tap Stop.
- **Quiet hours.** Keep alerts quiet on a schedule, with a minimum gap between alerts from one app.
- **Notification log.** Every notification with its app, time and message. Search it, filter by app, and tap an entry to jump to where it pointed.
- **Lighthouse look.** 16 colourways, your own colours and saved looks, 6 animated backgrounds, and 6 app icons.
- **23 languages.** Including right-to-left layouts.
- **Update pop-up.** Tells you when a new official version is out, with what's new. Settings also shows whether your copy is an official build.
